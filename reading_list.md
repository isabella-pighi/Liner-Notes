# Reading list: From Data Deluge to Data Strategy

A running library of articles, reports, and studies we are collecting on music data, streaming, fandom, and AI. This is the wide net: it holds things worth tracking, whether or not either paper cites them yet. When a piece is actually cited in a paper, it also appears in that paper's bibliography (`bibliography_part1.md` or `bibliography_part2.md`).

This work sits alongside "From Data Deluge to Data Strategy: Get the Power of Insights," presented by Isabella Pighi and Chiara Santoro at [SXSW 2025](https://schedule.sxsw.com/2025/events/PP153768).
